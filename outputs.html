GPR4Net Interactive Dashboard

Supplementary output · Accompanies a journal manuscript currently under revision

GPR4Net is a lightweight neural-network surrogate that predicts GPR B-scans in about 7 ms per sample, compared with roughly 102 s for an FDTD simulation. The dashboard demonstrates three predictive digital-twin scenarios: tunnel-lining inspection at 800 MHz and 600 MHz, and buried-pipeline detection at 1 GHz. Adjust subsurface parameters in the sidebar and the surrogate predicts the corresponding B-scan.
