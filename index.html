M-Twin4US

Maintenance-oriented Digital Twin for Underground Infrastructure

About the Project

M-Twin4US is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship developing a trustworthy digital twin framework for buried infrastructure. The project combines ground-penetrating radar sensing, Bayesian inversion, and uncertainty quantification to enable maintenance-oriented decision-making for underground utilities.
