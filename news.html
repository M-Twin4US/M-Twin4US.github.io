News

Conferences, workshops and updates from the project.

  • Apr 2026 Conference

    UKACM 2026 — Liverpool, UK

    The fellow gave a session keynote talk, “Predictive Maintenance of Underground Infrastructure with Ground Penetrating Radar: Integrating Hydro-Mechanical and Electro-Magnetic Modelling”, at the UKACM 2026 Conference, held at the University of Liverpool. The fellow also co-organised the mini-symposium “Advanced Numerical and Data-Driven Methods in Geotechnical Engineering” with fellow Marie Skłodowska-Curie researchers from the Digital Engineering team, Hoang-Giang Bui and Hassan Liravi.

  • Jan 2026 Conference

    TUST 40th Anniversary — Melbourne, Australia

    The fellow gave an invited talk, “Digital Twin for Predictive Maintenance of Underground Infrastructure”, at the 40th Anniversary Celebration of Tunnelling and Underground Space Technology (TUST), held at Monash University in Melbourne.

  • Jan 2026 Award

    Sustainability Impact Award at UIDC 2026

    At the 1st International Underground Infrastructure Digital Challenge (UIDC 2026) in Melbourne — held with the CTTU 2026 and TBM DiGs conferences — the fellow and team members Zehao Ye and Neng Wang submitted DOWSER and won the Sustainability Impact Award. The fellow presented the work, which was later published in Tunnelling and Underground Space Technology.

  • Dec 2025 Training & development

    Digital Engineering team end-of-year celebration

    As part of the career development supported by the host institution, the fellow took part in the Digital Engineering team’s end-of-year away day and celebration at Durham University, together with the advisor, Jelena Ninić, and fellow Marie Skłodowska-Curie researchers. Alongside its research, the team offers training in teaching, leadership and professional development.

  • Sep 2025 Conference

    EURO:TUN 2025 — Vienna, Austria

    The fellow gave a talk, “Utilizing Digital Twins for Predictive Maintenance of Underground Infrastructure”, at EURO:TUN 2025 (Computational Methods and Information Models in Tunnelling) in Vienna. Co-authors: Amanda Huang, Qianbing Zhang and Jelena Ninić.

  • Jul 2025 Conference

    IWAGPR 2025 — Thessaloniki, Greece

    The fellow presented “Utilizing GPR to Develop Digital Twins Across Maturity Levels for Underground Infrastructure Maintenance” at the 13th International Workshop on Advanced Ground Penetrating Radar (IWAGPR), held at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki.
